# Swim Tools v2 — handoff notes

Status as of 2026-10-15: committed and pushed on `development`; not yet merged to `master`, so not on GitHub Pages. Page: `/fitness-and-training/swim-tools-v2/`. It is a redesign of `/fitness-and-training/swim-tools/` (v1, left untouched), adapted from the "Race Deck" design export (`~/Downloads/2 · Race Deck — premium dark command center-html/CommandCenter.dc.html`) and restyled to the USMS brand. It is a design prototype, not a production mockup, so the `/mockup` "follow production" rules don't apply. Brand, project CSS rules and `modal.njk` still do.

## Layout

Every module lays out with CSS Grid, not production's BS3 columns (since 2026-10-15; an enhancement page, noted as an exception in CLAUDE.md): the Highlights band (`.swim-summary`), the Training Metrics tiles (`.swim-deck__kpis`), and the Race Planning and Time Conversions sections (`.swim-deck__grid`: one column below 992px; `--8-4` Race Planning, `--5-7` Time Conversions from 992px). Columns are 14px apart everywhere, rows 24px.

## Page structure

1. **Header**: "Swim Tools" and the greeting (the left 7 of 12 columns from tablet up, wrapping). Below it, a **toolbar** row of its own (`.swim-deck__toolbar`, right-aligned from tablet up, stacked on phones): the section tabs, then the sex and age-group pill when both are known ("50–54 Men's/Open", a solid blue 44px box with 8px corners and white text, as tall as a guest's controls). A guest's Age Group select and Competition Category choice take the pill's place, styled like Your Event's Distance, Stroke and Course controls (`.swim-deck__field`, `.swim-seg`), centred on the tabs, their labels for screen readers only. Age Group shows "Age" on grey until picked, then the age group on blue; neither category is pressed until picked, otherwise the Age Group select and/or the Competition Category choice.
1b. **Dashboard** (`#dashboard`; a section like the others, headed "Highlights", first of the sections on Home; logged-in members only, hidden and taken out of the sections for guests and for anyone logged in without a membership): its **summary band** (`Summary.njk`, `renderSummary`; since 2026-10-14, as v4's engagement cards), between the header and the sections, light grey with white cards. The goal and training status cards are primary. Age-up and comeback are secondary (`data-summary-fallback`). With no training status (nothing logged in the last 6 weeks), both show in full and the band is three cards. Beside a training status (`.swim-summary--status`), at most one shows, compact (`.swim-summary__card--mini`: its title, one short line or the next meet, one link): age-up when you move up an age group within 12 months, else comeback when your last race was 6 or more months ago (`SINCE_MONTHS`) or you haven't raced, else neither. Training Summary comes first, then Race Goal (since 2026-10-15). Desktop columns: Training Summary 4, Race Goal 6 and the compact card 2 of 12 (1200px and up; from 992px to 1199px the compact card is hidden), status 1 : goal 1.4 without one. Phones stack all of them.
   - **Goal** (two columns wide on desktop), for Your Event: latest and best there, the goal, time to go, and a track (slower on the left: filled latest, the best a solid blue triangle and the goal a solid deep yellow dot, as on the charts). The card shows a goal only once you've set one (typed in Race Planning's Goal): until then its headline is "Set a goal in the 50 free", Goal and To go are "—", and the track has no goal dot. Its link reads **Add goal**, then **Edit goal**, and scrolls to Race Planning's Goal. Beside Race Planning's Goal field, a text link: **Set this goal** takes the suggestion shown in its boxes (a guest's median) as your goal; once one is set, **Edit this goal** puts the cursor in its minutes. Once a goal is set, a line above the buttons says what it means: "Your race goal would be a personal best, dropping 0.31 s from your best time." (or ties your best; beats your latest by N s, with your best; is N s slower than your latest; or, with no races in the event, "Your first race in the 50 free will set your personal best."). Until you set one, v4's suggestion (just under the whole second below your latest, "break 2:55", or with no races in the event the age-group median) still stands in as Race Planning's goal: the Goal field's placeholders, Race Splits ("suggested goal 29.99") and Rank and Tier use it (`renderSummary` returns it to `render`); Race Time Predictions shows your predicted times, which a goal is meant to beat. Practice at goal pace jumps to Race Splits; Share progress card uses the share sheet or copies the line.
   - **Training status** (`renderStatus`): the last 7 days' yards against your weekly average over the last 6 weeks (averaged over fewer weeks if you started logging more recently), from Add to Log plus the demo swimmer's sample workouts; meters count as yards (× 1.0936). Status by that ratio: Overreaching (≥ 1.5, with a caution), Building (≥ 1.15), Maintaining (≥ 0.8), Recovering (above 0), Detraining (nothing in the last 7 days). Shows last 7 days, weekly average, swims, and a **timeline** (`renderTimeline`, since 2026-10-15): weekly yards for the last 12 weeks, 6 at a time, 80px tall (oldest left, the last 7 days in blue), with a 3-week rolling average line across them (the week and the 2 before it, over only the weeks since logging began, so it reads 14 weeks back) and a key below. ‹ › arrows either side step a week back or forward (back no further than the logging reaches); each week's first day sits under its bar, each bar's tooltip gives its dates, yards and average, and the card's "Last 6 weeks" becomes the dates shown once stepped back. One scale for all 12 weeks, so bars ease to their new heights as they step. The status figures stay on the current 6 weeks. (The fine print under the card, "Yards logged with Add to Log…", was removed the same day.) Re-renders after Add to Log. Sample math, not a published formula.
   - **Age-up** (secondary): your gap to the age-group median now and in your next age group, holding your latest time, with when you move up. Logged out: a prompt to log in.
   - **Comeback** (secondary): months since your last race, the next pool meet in Your Event's course (in your state when known) and the next virtual event (from `eventsEnriched`), See meets near you, and Add a race (opens the Add a Race modal).
2. **Section tabs** (`.swim-ribbon`, in the header): icon-only buttons with no circles, named by `aria-label` and `title`: Home (a paperclip, 18.5px, in place of a house) / Race Planning (a stopwatch, 23px) / Time Conversions (two arrows, 22px) / Training Metrics (a dumbbell: two outlined plates joined by a heavy bar, cleaned up from the designer's Illustrator export, its colour following the tab's; narrowed 2026-10-15 by shortening the bar from 14.84 to 11 units, viewBox 41.14 × 45, shown 32 × 35px so the plates keep their size). The icons are inline SVG strokes, `stroke-width: 2.4` in their own 24-unit grid (the dumbbell sets its own widths). The selected one (`aria-pressed`) is USMS blue, the rest medium-light grey (#a6a6a6, darker grey on hover). Home is selected on load and puts the Dashboard back on top and the sections in page order. Clicking a section **reorders** the page so it comes first, above even the Dashboard; the Dashboard then sits between it and the other sections. While sticky behavior is mothballed (below), the toolbar never sticks, so a click only reorders. With it on: clicked from the stuck toolbar (any tab, the selected one included), the page smooth-scrolls, keeping the toolbar stuck, until the chosen section's heading sticks onto the toolbar row (phones: until it sits just under the toolbar); instant with reduced motion. Near the top, with the toolbar in place, nothing scrolls. Page order (since 2026-10-13): Race Planning, Time Conversions, then Training Metrics, for logged-in members. Anyone else (a guest, or logged in without a membership) gets Time Conversions, Training Metrics, then Race Planning, with the tabs in that order too, and no Home tab (with no summary it would only repeat that order); Time Conversions is selected on load (`isMember`).
   - **All sticky behavior is mothballed (2026-10-15): the page scrolls naturally.** `STICKY = false` in swim-tools-v2.js switches off the script (the toolbar's stuck state and shadow, stuck headings, tabs following the scroll, the pager), and the sticky CSS is commented out under `MOTHBALLED` notes; the 50px gap between sections is kept. With the toolbar never stuck, a tab click only reorders the sections. To restore, set `STICKY` to true and uncomment the CSS. What it did, kept for reference:
   - **Section pager** (tablet and up, since 2026-10-15; `pageOn`, `pinPanels`): scrolling down, each section holds still once its heading docks (its panel CSS-sticky for `--sd-hold`, 100px of scrolling, the room at the section's foot; a negative margin keeps the visible gap between sections at 50px), then the page glides on to the next section, heading docked. A panel taller than the window (Race Planning) pins when its foot reaches the window's (`--sd-pin-top`, kept current by a ResizeObserver), so it scrolls through first. Scrolling up is unchanged, the last section scrolls on to the footer, and a tab click's scroll counts as a glide so the pager doesn't divert it. The next section, when it's in view below a short one, still moves during the hold.

## Behavior rules worth keeping

- **Race Time Predictions** works as v4's (since 2026-10-13), with the v4 styling under `swim-pred*` classes:
  - **Data**: your age group's USMS rankings from the Top Times files (`/data/top-times/`, `docs/api/top-times.openapi.yaml`), fetched per event as the card needs them (`groupRank`), using `available.json` (fetched at start). A course with no files uses SCY's, converted. Age group is the profile's or the select's; gender is the persona's (`swimmerGender`), and with no gender there's no ranking. Synthetic rankings add "(synthetic data)" in very light grey (`.swim-synth`, `setWithSynth`) to the predictions and Rank and Tier subtitles, the age-up card's note, and Race History's note (naming the seasons).
  - **Chart**: pace per 100 by distance, slower higher. Age-group lines: fastest (1st), top ten (10th), median and slowest (outliers dropped, `typicalSlowest`), with grey fills beneath. Your bests in your current age group are triangles, solid line between neighboring distances. Unraced distances are projected (open dots, dotted line): an older swim age-adjusted (`ageAdjust`), else the same distance in another course converted, else your nearest distance scaled by how the median slows (`project`). Your Event's label is bold. A "Projected Winner" pill and note show where your time is as fast as this season's fastest.
  - **Skeleton** while the rankings load (`.is-loading` on the card, when there are no rankings yet and `pending()`): a grey shimmer behind the plot (your own dots still show over it), grey bars shaped like the summary note (a kicker, a headline, then three paragraph lines on desktop; two headline and five paragraph lines on phones) and grey pills in place of the legend, sized so the card doesn't change height when the data arrives (exact on desktop, within a few px on phones). Static when motion is reduced; screen readers hear "Loading USMS rankings…". Skeletons are new to the site (production uses spinners).
  - **No races to predict from** (a guest, or a stroke you haven't raced): predictions come from Your Event's time instead, your typed goal if any, else the age-group median, scaled to each distance by how the median changes (`fromTime`). Untyped, they are the medians. The summary says "These are the default median times for 45–49. For better results, add your age and category." (the link opens Swimmer Info) while a guest is still on the default Age Group and Competition Category, then "Projected times are based on the Women's 55–59 median. Add a race to adjust your projections." once they've picked both (and for anyone with no races in the stroke); typed, it says "Predicted from your 50 free time of 35.00". The table labels them "median" or "predicted"; Race Splits and Rank and Tier still name an untyped time as the median.
  - **Legend pills** turn each line off and on (`state.predHidden`). **Table view** shows every value with its pace.
  - **Your goal**: once you've typed a goal in Race Planning, a solid deep yellow dot (#e0a100, white-ringed, `.swim-pred__dot.is-goal`) at Your Event's distance, with a "Your goal: 28.50 in the 50 free" tooltip and a "Your goal" key in the legend; it counts in the chart's scale. A goal at or under this season's fastest in your age group also flies the yellow **Projected Winner** pill (above the dot; below it near the chart's top), and the yellow note says "Your goal of 20.50 in the 50 free beats this season's fastest in men 50–54 (21.66)."
  - **No recent races of yours here** (a swimmer with races, but none in the last 8 years in this course and stroke): the projections from their other courses stand, and the note says so: "Projected from your SCY and LCM free races. Race SCM free to see your own times here." or, with older races here, "… Your SCM breast races are from 2009–11, over 8 years ago, so they aren't used." (Race History, meanwhile, is blank with "You haven't raced SCM free yet." when there are none at all.)
  - **Summary** under the chart, always a note (`.swim-pred-insight--note`: blue left edge, kicker, headline, paragraph): Your strengths (where your pace is closest to and furthest from the median), Your races (one distance raced, or none), Your time (predicted from a typed time), Age-group medians (guests and no races), or Age-group rankings (none available). The "races over 8 years ago" note opens the paragraph.
  - No goal line any more. Your Event's default time (splits, goal placeholders) is your best, else your projected time, else the median.
- **Training Effect** (Training Metrics, `renderTraining`, `TrainingEffect.njk`; replaced Relative Effort 2026-10-14): what the Your Swim workout does for your fitness, 0.0–5.0, on the swim-TSS model. Sample math, not a published formula.
  - **Threshold pace** (`thresholdPace`): freestyle at your ~30-minute pace (the 1650 yd / 1500 m), from your nearest real free race in your current age group (in the swim's course, or another course converted), scaled by Riegel's 1.06; else the nearest age-adjusted older swim; else a projection; else the age-group median.
  - **Load** (`swimLoad`): intensity = threshold pace ÷ the swim's freestyle-equivalent pace (`STROKE_PACE`: Back 1.10, Breast 1.22, Fly 1.12, IM 1.12); load = intensity³ × hours × 100 × the stroke's cost (`STROKE_LOAD`: Back 0.95, Breast 1.05, Fly 1.15, IM 1.05). An hour at threshold is 100.
  - **Locked until 5 logged swims** in the last 6 weeks (`TE_MIN_SWIMS`, sample workouts included): the tile shows a faded, blurred sample reading behind an upsell (`.swim-te-lock`). Guests and non-members: "See what each swim does for you", members get it for every swim, **Join U.S. Masters Swimming**. Members: "Log N more swims to unlock", five progress dots ("2 of 5 logged") and **Add to Log**.
  - **Effect**: 5 × (1 − e^(−0.7 × load ÷ typical)), where typical is your average logged swim's load over the last 42 days (Add to Log, `swimTools.log.<persona>`), or 55 with no logged swims (about an hour of moderate swimming). **Sample workouts** (24 modeled swims before the Garmin ones since 2026-10-15, `SAMPLE_SWIMS_MODELED`: 8 weeks building from 2,300 to 5,200 yards a week, then an easy week, for the 12-week timeline; `sampleLog`): Jay Place (KT2JH), the demo swimmer, gets 19 sample pool swims when logged in (`SAMPLE_SWIMS`: yards and moving times from a real Garmin export, May to Sep 2020, 450 to 2,200 yd at about 1:40 to 2:07 per 100), kept in order but re-dated three a week (Mon, Wed, Sat, `SAMPLE_DAYS`) back from today so he always reads as a current, active swimmer. SCY (the export has no pool length), no stroke or effort, added to anything he logs and flagged `synthetic`; the summary says "including sample workouts". Only for his own estimates, never shown as races. No other persona has any. A typical swim scores about 2.5, Steady. Bands (`TE_WORDS`, effort words like the Effort slider's): Easy, Light (1), Steady (2), Solid (3), Hard push (4), **Peak effort** (4.5, `TE_PEAK`; about 3× your typical load or more), which adds a caution: "About N times your typical load: you may be overdoing it. Take an easy day or two after, and build toward swims like this gradually."
  - The tile shows its icon and name, the working (load against typical and how many logged swims, percent of threshold pace, and where the threshold came from), then the score and its band. No bar (removed 2026-10-14). "You rated it 7/10" still shows beside the score when Effort is rated; the rating doesn't change it.
- **Column balance** (`balanceRacePlanning`, `.swim-deck__grid--balance`): on desktop, Race Planning's two columns end level. After each render (and on a width change, and once fonts load) it measures both at natural height; if the right column is taller it shows more Your Races rows, then shortens the Race Splits window; if the left is taller it lengthens the splits window (when it scrolls), then shows fewer Your Races rows (not under 3, `RACES_MIN`; the splits window not under 5 rows, `SPLITS_MIN_ROWS`). The last card in each column stretches to take up what's left (under a row). Off when the columns stack, and while Your Races shows all.
- **Race History** (`RaceTrend.njk`, `renderTrend`; ported from v4's Race History chart 2026-10-14), above Race Time Predictions, then Your Races: your races in Your Event's course and stroke as a line per event over time, pace per 100 up the side (slower higher, unit above), each race a dot. Your Event's line is wide and see-through with its dots labelled (up to 40, `LABEL_LIMIT`; else on hover); the stroke's other distances are thin and faint (solid); each event's best is a solid ▲. Two grey lines mark the Top 10 (legend key "Top 10 (1st–10th place)"): the fastest swim (solid) and 10th place (lighter, dashed) in your age group each season (your age on May 31 of that season; `groupRank` takes an optional season for this), where those rankings exist. A typed goal (Race Planning's Goal) shows as the same solid deep yellow dot as Race Time Predictions', at today's date and the goal's pace (so the years run to now), with a "Your goal" key and a light grey dashed line to it from Your Event's latest race; not with every race showing. The legend has no pill per event (retired 2026-10-15): a "Selected race" key (Your Event's wide, see-through line), the Top 10 pill (which still turns those lines off and on), Personal Best, Your goal (once set); Show All Races puts every race on the chart (no pills, no unit, no Top 10 line). Over five years of races: zoom (− +, down to 5 years), pan (‹ ›, half a view), All years, and drag the chart sideways when zoomed (a hint on touch screens until the first drag). With races, but none in Your Event's course and stroke, the card stays with its chart blank and "You haven't raced SCM free yet." in red over it (Show All Races still offered); hidden only with no dated races at all. State in `trend` (all, hidden, zoom), not saved.

- **Rank and Tier** (since 2026-10-14, as v4's; `renderTier`, `RankTier.njk`), below Race Splits: Your Event's time (your goal, else best, projected time or the age-group median, the same time Race Splits uses) on your age group's latest Top Times ranking: a Fastest reference row, then Top 10, 11th–25%, 26–50%, 51–75% and 76–100% with each tier's cut, yours outlined; then the next rung, where the time would place, and how far to go. Non-members (not logged in, or no membership tier) see it blurred under a join prompt. Grey rungs while rankings load. Its link to Top 10 by Decade is retired with that card.
- **Guests** (logged out, e.g. `?user=NEW`; since 2026-10-14): the age group starts on **45–49** (since 2026-10-14; also the default for logged-in swimmers with no birth date). It was briefly 60–64, the largest men's group in USMS membership (3,472 of 29,142 male members, 11.9%, Nov. 1, 2024). Race Planning opens on the 50 free SCY with its age-group median filled into the Time boxes as a real value (`state.goalAuto`); until the guest types their own time, it follows the header's age group and the event. Time Conversions also opens on the 50 free SCY but empty (just the `mm : ss . hh` / `mm : ss` placeholders). While untouched, the time shows grey and regular weight like a placeholder (`.swim-time.is-default`), then dark and bold once typed. Race Splits and Rank and Tier name it as the median ("45–49 median …"). Your Swim keeps its 1650 sample. A **Competition Category** choice (Men's/Open / Women's, a `.swim-seg`, `state.sex` = Men or Women, starting on Men's/Open) sits after the Age Group select whenever the page doesn't know your sex (logged out, or no gender on the profile); it sets whose rankings every card uses (`swimmerGender`), and the header pill follows it when the age is known. For a guest, Age Group starts grey (`.is-unset`) and Competition Category with neither option pressed (rankings still use Men's/Open meanwhile), each turning blue once picked (`state.ageChosen`, `state.sexChosen`; picking the value already showing counts); until both are picked, Race Time Predictions' projected dots, labels and line are grey too (`.is-pending`, `guestPending`).
- **Which races count** (since 2026-10-13, v2 and v4 alike): only the last 8 years' (`WINDOW_YEARS`; undated races count). Within that, races from your current age group are real bests (`inAgeGroup`; races without an age count as current), and earlier ones are age-adjusted into it. A real current-group swim beats an adjusted older one at the same distance, even a faster one. If none of the window's races are in Your Event's stroke, every race in the stroke is used, age-adjusted, and the subtitle and insight say they're from races over 8 years ago (`predPool`). The insight compares real and age-adjusted swims, not times projected from other distances.
- **Top 10 by Decade** (`renderTopTens`; retired 2026-10-14, notes kept for when it returns):
  - **Its own Stroke and Distance** filters sit in the card header (`data-tens` / `data-tens-input`, `state.tens`), with distances in the lists' course (SCY).
  - **Following Your Event**: the card starts on Your Event, and switches back to it whenever Your Event's course, distance or stroke changes (`followEvent`). A meters event maps to its yards equivalent (400 m → 500 yd) through `convertRace`. The subtitle says "Your event" while the two match.
  - **Columns**: one per season, each a bar from 1st to 10th with every time as a dot. 1st is solid and labelled bold above the bar, and 10th is labelled below it. A pale band joins the seasons, with a line along the 1st-place times. Fastest is at the top, the reverse of the predictor (where slower is higher).
  - **No line for your own time.** It was removed at the user's request, along with its row of places, and so were the race/estimate on Your Event and the "Your best" on other events.
  - **Goal**: a dashed line plus a `Goal` place row, shown only on Your Event when a goal is set.
  - **Which list**: the one for the swimmer's age group and gender. With none, it falls back to the first list and a note says so.
  - **Notes**: one says when your goal was converted to SCY, and another says when the card is showing a fallback list.
  - **Size**: the viewBox is 960 wide, not 720, because the card spans the row. Its height grows by 16 when the goal's place row shows. Below 720px it scrolls inside the card.
- **Personas**:
  - **Which persona**: the active one is `?user=`, then `sessionStorage.activeUser`, then the page's `swimmerId` (KT2JH). This is the same resolution order as `current-user.js`.
  - **What it sets on load**:
    - Age and Age group: today's age from `users.json` `birthDate` (`ageOn`).
    - Your Event: the event they've swum most, with the latest winning a tie (`startFromSwimmer`).
  - **No race history** (e.g. `?user=COACH`): empty races, averages only.
- **Saved races**: races added or removed are stored per persona in localStorage `swimTools.races.<id>`, which then replaces that persona's seed list. The old unscoped `swimTools.races` key is ignored. New races get an `age` computed from the birth date.
- **Time fields** (`TimeField.njk` macro):
  - Three inputs `mm : ss . hh` in one outlined box; paces have no hundredths. Clicking into the box starts at minutes.
  - Typing auto-advances between boxes.
  - Up/Down arrows, the wheel, or a vertical swipe (one step per 24px; the page doesn't scroll meanwhile) step the focused box. Seconds carry into minutes like a clock: down from 00 gives 59 and takes a minute off, up from 59 gives 00 and adds one; at 0:00, down does nothing. Minutes stop at 0 and 59; hundredths wrap 0–99.
  - Focusing a box selects its contents.
  - Leaving a box pads it (5 seconds → 05, 5 hundredths → 50). **Leaving an empty hundredths box fills it with `00`** if minutes or seconds have a value.
  - Empty fields show placeholders: the suggested goal for Race Planning's Goal, the prediction elsewhere.
- **Distance changes**: the distance always snaps to a real event for the course and stroke (`fitDistance`), keeping yards/meters equivalents (500↔400, 1000↔800, 1650↔1500).
- **Changing Your Event** (course, distance or stroke) clears the goal.
- **Swimmer Info modal** (`swimDeckWho`, Editors.njk; `prepareWho`/`renderWho`): a guest's Age Group Conversion prompt, until they've picked both age group and competition category, is a primary button, "Start Here" (its aria-label says what it's for), opening a modal with an Age Group select ("Select" until picked) and the Competition Category choice. It starts from anything already picked in the header and applies on **Confirm**, which stays disabled (faded) until both are picked, exactly as picking both in the header would. After that the prompt is "Enter a time or a pace above."
- **Modals** use the project `modal.njk` with `modalModifier="swim-deck-modal"`. The page JS adds Escape, closing on a backdrop click, and returning focus to the opener.

- **Source**: parsed from the user's saved USMS individual results pages (`comp/meets/indresults.php?SwimmerID=…`, saved to `~/Downloads/USMS Individual Meet Results for *.html`). The parser is a scratch Python script and isn't kept. To redo it:
  - Split the page on `<a name="SCY|LCM|SCM">`.
  - Read the 8 cells of each `<tr align="left" valign="top">` row.
  - Drop relay and split swims (`Place == Split`), DQ/EXH entries, events the tools don't offer (25s) and exact duplicates.
  - Every row on the four pages was accounted for.
- **Getting the pages**: usms.org results pages sit behind an **Azure WAF that returns 403 to curl and WebFetch, even over the VPN**. Don't try to get around it. Ask the user to save the page from their browser.
- **Top 10 lists** (`topTens.json` → `{ _comment, lists: [ { course, gender: Men|Women, ageGroup (youngest age), ageGroupLabel, seasons: { <year>: { <stroke>: { <distance>: [times, fastest first] } } } } ] }`): parsed from the user's saved Top 10 individual-event pages (`~/Downloads/<year> USMS Top 10 SCY for Men 50-54.html`). Each event is an `<h3 id="Event…">` followed by a `table.toptenlist`, and the time is the 6th cell. Ties make some lists 11 long, and one is 9. 1976 has no 1000 Free, so that column shows "No list". Only times are kept, no names. To add an age group, gender or course, save its Top 10 pages, parse them the same way and append an entry to `lists`. No JS change is needed.
- **Estimated birth dates** for Rick (05/15/1950) and Tracy (04/01/1957) come from their results ages. The SCM age column is age on Dec 31, so ignore SCM rows when working out birth dates. Their gender is left blank.
- **Kyle's birth date may be a year off.** `users.json` has 06/15/1983, but his results suggest mid-1984.
- **Key collision**: `SHARK` is both Rick's users.json key and the Sarasota Sharks club abbreviation in `clubsLocal.json`. These don't conflict (`?user=` vs `?club=`), but keep it in mind.
- **Sample factors**: the course factors (`COURSES`), the age-group slowdown factors (`AGE_GROUPS`), calorie constants and the rank model (`RANK_*`) are all sample values, not USMS data. The footer disclaimer says race history is real and averages are sample data.

## Styling rules learned the hard way

- **Production `section{display:table}`** breaks widths and made the page overflow to 612px on mobile. `.swim-deck__section` and `.swim-deck-page` are forced to `display:block`.
- **Production `.usms-container p/li` sizes** win over page styles unless selectors are prefixed with `.swim-deck`. Grid items also need `width:100%; justify-self:stretch`.
- **Production `.btn-md`** is `height: 32px !important`.
- **Rem base is 10px** (`html` font-size).
- **Specificity**: the error color needs `.swim-deck .swim-deck__note.swim-deck__note-error`.
- **Baseline alignment** of the tile numbers uses `margin-bottom: -0.167em` on the units.
- **Colors** live in tokens on the root (`--sd-blue #0068ad`, `--sd-red`, `--sd-orange #ff7f00`, `--sd-tint` light blue, …). `--sd-yellow` is currently unused.
- **No inline `<style>`/`<script>`** in templates. JSON data islands are fine.

## How to check it

- **Build** to scratch without touching the user's `_site/`: `npx @11ty/eleventy --output=<scratchpad>/site`.

- **Drive it** with the project's Playwright via `NODE_PATH=$PWD/node_modules node script.js`. Don't install browsers.
- **Measure pixels**, when needed, with Python PIL.
- **Visual regression**: the page isn't in `tests/usms-visual-regression-screenshots/pages.js`, so the visual suite doesn't cover it.
